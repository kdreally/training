# U14 Rubric

Visible to learners. Total **20 points**.

| Criterion | Points | Must-have? | Evidence |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| At least three examples with concrete inputs and outputs, including a zero or a returned-loan edge | 6 | Yes | spec |
| At least four non-goals naming real temptations (HTTP, dependency, new file, rename, fuzzy match, dates, or similar) | 6 | Yes | spec |
| "Intuitive" and "if that's easy" do not survive as the behavior | 3 | Yes | spec |
| Files and Done are present and consistent with the non-goals | 3 | Yes | spec |
| Answers explain temptations and show one rejected bad example | 2 | No | `answers.md` |

### Partial credit

- Examples: 4/6 if outputs are described in words ("it fails") without the error string or the number.
- Non-goals: "do not write bugs" does not count toward the four.

### Must-have vs nice-to-have

Answers are nice-to-have. Missing must-have caps at 15/20.
