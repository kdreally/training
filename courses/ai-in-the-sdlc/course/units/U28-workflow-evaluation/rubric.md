### Partial credit

- Sheet marks changelog as pass because "there is a line": cap the sheet row at 5/8.
- Next action "prompt a fix in place": cap overall at 1/4.

### Must-have vs nice-to-have

All rows must-have. Standing-rule rows are included in the spirit of the sheet; if the six spec rows are strong and standing rows are missing, deduct 2 points from the sheet row rather than failing the whole unit only for that. Still require overall fail.
