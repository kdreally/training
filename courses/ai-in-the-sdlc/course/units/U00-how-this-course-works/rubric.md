# U00 Rubric

Visible to learners. Total **20 points**.

| Criterion | Points | Must-have? | Evidence |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| Explains a unit and three of its parts with a reason for each | 5 | Yes | `answers.md` Q1 |
| Distinguishes practice from assignment, including the human assessor | 4 | Yes | `answers.md` Q2 |
| Seven stuck-protocol steps are present and in a usable order | 5 | Yes | `answers.md` Q3 |
| The filled-in stuck example quotes the unknown phrase and refuses to skip ahead | 3 | Yes | `answers.md` Q3 |
| Explains why graded work is files, not a live model reply | 2 | No | `answers.md` Q4 |
| Filenames match the assignment | 1 | Yes | folder |

### Partial credit

- Q1: 3/5 if the definition is right but only one part is justified.
- Q3 steps: 3/5 if five or six steps are present and the missing ones are not the "write what you tried" step.
- Q3 example: 1/3 if they ask a chatbot immediately and also mention the protocol.
- Do not penalize imperfect English. Do penalize a paste of this README with the examples unchanged.

### Must-have vs nice-to-have

Failing any must-have row caps the grade at 15/20 and that row is "revise and resubmit." Q4 may be thin without failing the unit.
