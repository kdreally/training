# U00 Assessor notes

Assessor-only. Do not link from the learner README.

## Model answer sketch

**Q1:** A unit is one folder teaching one idea. "Why this exists" gives the problem before the technique. The worked example shows a complete sample. The rubric shows how a human will score the submission.

**Q2:** Practice is rehearsal; wrong answers are allowed. The assignment is evidence submitted to a human trainer who marks the rubric.

**Q3 steps:** name the last clear point; quote the first unclear point; re-read vocabulary; re-read the example; change one thing; write what you tried; ask the trainer with those notes.

**Q3 example:** They should name "standing instructions" as the unknown, note that a later unit will define it, and wait rather than collecting a chatbot definition they cannot yet judge.

**Q4:** Model replies vary, so two learners with the same skill would get different "results." Files (rules, specs, prompts, reviews) are stable evidence.

## Common weak submissions

- Calls the assignment "practice" and the rubric "optional."
- Stuck protocol reduced to "ask the AI."
- Q4 says live replies are graded because they prove the tool works. That misses the point of the course.

## Partial credit

A learner who lists the steps in a slightly different order still passes Q3 if each step is recognisable and they do not skip "write what you tried."
