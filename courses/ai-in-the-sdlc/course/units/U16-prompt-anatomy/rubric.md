# U16 Rubric

Visible to learners. Total **20 points**.

| Criterion | Points | Must-have? | Evidence |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| Prompt has target, fences, verify, and stop | 6 | Yes | `prompts/already-borrowed.txt` |
| Fences name allowed files and forbid `package.json` or new dependencies; tests are required, not optional | 5 | Yes | prompt |
| No smuggled route, rename, or new error wording | 3 | Yes | prompt |
| Bad prompt's smuggled decisions are listed and absent from the rewrite | 4 | Yes | `answers.md` Q2 |
| Rename is described as a future spec, not a prompt aside | 2 | No | `answers.md` Q3 |

### Partial credit

- Four parts: 1.5 points each, round to a whole number. "Be careful" does not count as a fence.
- Q2: 2/4 if they rewrite politely but keep "no tests unless you have time."

### Must-have vs nice-to-have

Q3 is nice-to-have. Missing must-have caps at 15/20.
