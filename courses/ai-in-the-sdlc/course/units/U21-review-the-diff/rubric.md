Visible to learners. Total **20 points**.

| Criterion | Points | Must-have? | Evidence |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| Five steps present; decision is restore | 6 | Yes | `review.md` |
| Citations include `package.json` and the missing tests | 4 | Yes | `review.md` |

### Partial credit

- Decision accept: the five-step row scores at most 2/6, because the lesson's diff is a restore. The unit is revise-and-resubmit if they accept.
- Citations: 2/4 if only lodash is named and tests are never mentioned.

### Must-have vs nice-to-have

Q2–Q3 are nice-to-have. An accept decision fails the must-have decision row and caps at 15/20.
