# U02 Rubric

Visible to learners. Total **20 points**.

| Criterion | Points | Must-have? | Evidence |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| Definition includes folder, edit, and CLI, plus three accurate "is not" statements | 6 | Yes | Q1 |
| Four tasks labeled with a reason that matches the work product | 6 | Yes | Q2 |
| Chat-paste failure mentions a concrete risk (stale file, invented style, or missed tests) | 4 | Yes | Q3 |
| Responsibility picture keeps acceptance with the human | 3 | Yes | Q4 |
| Filenames correct | 1 | Yes | folder |

### Partial credit

- Q1: 4/6 if the definition is right and only one "is not" is real.
- Q2: the "find which test" task may be `chat` **or** `agent` if the reason is coherent (paste the test file, or search the folder). Award full credit for either. `neither` is wrong.
- Q4: 1/3 if they say the agent may accept its own work when tests pass. Tests inform the human; they do not replace the human in this course.

### Must-have vs nice-to-have

All rows are must-have. A miss caps the grade at 15/20.
