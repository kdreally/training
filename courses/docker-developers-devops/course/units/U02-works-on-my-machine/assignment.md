# U02 Assignment — Diagnose the drift

Submit the following to your trainer in **one folder or zip** named:

`U02-YourName` (use your real name or student id as they prefer)

## Files to submit

### 1. `incident.md`

Answer these prompts in your own words. Do not paste the lesson back unchanged.

1. **Tell the story.** Describe a real or realistic "works on my machine" incident in 6–9 lines. It can be from your own experience, a story you were told, or a realistic invention. Name the people, what each machine had, and what went wrong.
2. **Name the environment.** For your incident, list each of the five environment parts (operating system, runtime, libraries, versions, configuration) and say which one caused the trouble. If a part was fine, say so and why.
3. **Why "install the same thing" fails.** In 3–5 lines, explain why telling someone to reinstall does not reliably fix this class of problem.
4. **Predict then read.** Before looking back at the worked example, a teammate's app fails with:
   `Error: connect ECONNREFUSED 127.0.0.1:5432`
   Write what you think this message means. Then write, in two lines, what the lesson said it meant. Note whether your prediction matched.
5. **Drift detection.** Write three questions a team could ask to catch version drift *before* it causes a bad bug.
6. **Explain in your own words.** Define *environment*, *version drift*, and *reproducibility* in your own words as if explaining to a friend who is not technical.

### 2. `checklist.md`

Copy this checklist and mark each item `[x]` when true:

## Definition of done

- Both files are present with the exact names above.
- `incident.md` answers all six prompts in your own words.
- The environment is described with at least four of the five parts named.
- The prediction in Q4 is written down *before* the explanation, and both are present.
- The checklist is completed honestly.
