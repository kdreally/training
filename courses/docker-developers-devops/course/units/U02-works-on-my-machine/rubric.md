# U02 Rubric

Visible to learners. Total **20 points**.

| Criterion | Points | Must-have? | Evidence |
|-----------|--------|------------|----------|
| Incident is coherent and shows the two machines differing | 4 | Yes | `incident.md` Q1 |
| Environment named with four or more of the five parts | 4 | Yes | `incident.md` Q2 |
| Correct cause identified (unmatched, not merely missing) | 3 | Yes | `incident.md` Q2 |
| Explains why reinstall does not guarantee a match | 2 | Yes | `incident.md` Q3 |
| Prediction recorded and compared to the decoded error | 3 | No | `incident.md` Q4 |
| Three concrete drift-detection questions | 2 | No | `incident.md` Q5 |
| All three terms defined in plain, correct language | 2 | Yes | `incident.md` Q6 |
| Files named correctly; checklist present and honest | 0 (gate) | Yes | folder / zip structure |

Note: the final row is a **gate**, not points. Missing or misnamed files must be flagged to the learner, but the total stays out of 20.

### Partial credit notes (assessors)

- Q2: award 2/4 if three parts are named; 1/4 if only "the OS" is blamed with no detail. Blaming the operating system by itself is a thoughtful but incomplete answer — award partial and point to libraries/versions.
- Q3: award 1/2 for "installs can differ" without the word version; full marks if they connect it to matching versions, not just presence.
- Q4: award 1/3 if a prediction exists but is vague; 3/3 if it distinguishes "connection refused" from "wrong password." A wrong-but-reasoned prediction earns full marks here.
- Q6: definitions must be in their own words. A near-verbatim copy of the vocabulary table earns at most 1/2.
- Do not penalize imperfect English. Penalize empty answers and pasted lesson text.
