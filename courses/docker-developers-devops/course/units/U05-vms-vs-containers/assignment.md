# U05 Assignment — Choose honestly

Submit the following to your trainer in **one folder or zip** named:

`U05-YourName` (use your real name or student id as they prefer)

## Files to submit

### 1. `comparison.md`

Build a comparison of virtual machines and containers with these rows: *contains, own operating system?, size, startup time, isolation mechanism, best suited to.* Fill in each cell in your own words. Do not paste the lesson's table unchanged; a rewritten table in your own phrasing earns full marks.

Below the table, answer:

1. **Explain in your own words.** Define *hypervisor*, *kernel*, *container engine*, and *isolation* as if to a friend who is not technical.
2. **The key difference.** In two or three sentences, explain the single structural difference between a VM and a container and why it leads to the size and speed differences.
3. **Scenario choices.** For each scenario, choose **VM** or **container** and give one sentence of justification:
   - a) Twenty small services that start and stop many times an hour.
   - b) Running macOS software on a Linux server.
   - c) Running a stranger's untrusted code with maximum separation.
   - d) A team wanting identical app environments on mixed laptops.
4. **Debug this claim.** A colleague writes: "Containers are just smaller virtual machines, so they are always more secure and always the right choice." Write two or three sentences correcting the mistakes in that claim.
5. **Predict then read.** Before re-reading the lesson, list **two** situations where replacing all VMs with containers would fail or be risky. Then note whether the lesson agreed.
